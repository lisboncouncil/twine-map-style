# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-20)

- Water in a tint of Melon Orange (`#fbcfc6`), shores and canals in Melon Orange, water labels in dark Red Orange.
- Parks and woods in tints of Light Steel Blue, park labels in Slate Blue, so that they stay apart from the warm water.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-20)

- First public release: TWINE style (33 layers, OpenMapTiles schema), glyphs for Raleway Bold, Raleway ExtraBold, Albert Sans Regular and Albert Sans SemiBold, preview page with the three pilots and an event venue.
